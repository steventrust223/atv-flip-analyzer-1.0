📊Understanding Deal Classes

HOT High margin, manageable risk
These are the best opportunities. High profit potential with acceptable risk. Action: Move fast!

SOLID Good deal, standard risk
Profitable deals worth pursuing. May require more negotiation or work. Action: Pursue actively.

MARGINAL OK deal or good deal with high risk
Could be profitable but has concerns. Action: Monitor or negotiate harder.

PASS Not worth pursuing
Poor profit potential, too risky, or overpriced. Action: Skip unless situation changes.

💰MAO Calculation

The Maximum Allowable Offer (MAO) is calculated based on:

MAO = (Retail Value × Offer %) - Repair Costs - Minimum Profit

Offer Percentages (default)

  • Runners: 65% of retail value
  • Projects: 45% of retail value
  • Frames/Rollers: 25% of retail value

The Offer Target is MAO minus a negotiation cushion (default 10%), giving you room to come up during negotiation.

💡 Tip: Adjust percentages in Settings based on your market and experience. Start conservative and adjust as you learn your local market.

⚠️Risk Factors

The Risk Score (0-100) considers:

  • Condition severity and running status
  • Hazard flags (no title, blown motor, frame damage)
  • Distance from you
  • Complexity of required work
  • Unknown factors

Hazard Flags

  • NO_TITLE - Budget $150-300 for bonded title
  • BLOWN_MOTOR - Major rebuild or swap needed
  • FRAME_DAMAGE - Often a deal-breaker
  • PARTS_MISSING - Factor in sourcing time & cost
  • UNKNOWN_HISTORY - Inspect thoroughly

📈Sales Velocity

Velocity scores indicate how fast units typically sell:

  • A - Very Fast: Sport quads, youth ATVs, popular dirt bikes
  • B - Normal: Standard utility ATVs, common models
  • C - Slow: Older models, niche vehicles
  • D - Very Slow: Overpriced, obscure brands
💡 Tip: Fast-selling categories let you turn capital faster. Consider velocity when you have limited capital.

🔧Parts & Builds

Track parts needed for each project in ATV_PARTS_NEEDED:

  • Link parts to specific ATV IDs
  • Track status: Needed → Sourcing → Ordered → Received → Installed
  • Parts costs roll up to update total project cost

Use ATV_BUILD_PLANS to save templates for common builds like "Top End Rebuild" or "Full Resto".

🎯Best Practices

  • Run analysis daily when actively sourcing
  • Act fast on HOT deals - they go quick
  • Verify listings in person before committing
  • Track actual results in POST_SALE_TRACKER to improve estimates
  • Adjust settings based on your actual experience
  • Focus on what you know - specialize in specific makes/models
💡 Pro Tip: Banshees, Raptors, and YFZ450s have strong resale markets. Youth ATVs sell fast but watch for hidden damage from crashes.
